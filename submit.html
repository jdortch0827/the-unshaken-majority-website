Submit a Case

The strongest submissions include the original source, a genuinely comparable case, dates, context, and a clear explanation of the suspected inconsistency.

Do not submit: confidential employer files, illegally obtained records, private medical information, unredacted identification numbers, home addresses, threats, or material that could put someone in danger.

You may use an alias, but provide a working email if you want a response.

This first version opens your email app with the form filled in. Review it and attach evidence before sending.
