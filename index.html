The Same Standard Test

One framework for every side

Every investigation follows the same core questions, regardless of political party, race, religion, sex, institution, company, or ideology.

  • What exactly happened, and what is the strongest available evidence?
  • What rule, policy, principle, or public standard is being claimed?
  • Is the comparison genuinely similar, or are important facts different?
  • Was the difference explained, and is that explanation reasonable?
  • What consistent standard should apply going forward?

Accountability

Credibility requires rules

We do not promise to be right every time. We promise to show our work, separate fact from opinion, invite meaningful responses, and make visible corrections.

Source-first

Primary records are preferred. Headlines, clips, and viral posts are treated as leads—not proof.

Right of response

When practical, organizations and people criticized in a case will be given a fair opportunity to respond.

Public corrections

Meaningful errors will be corrected clearly, with the date and substance of the correction preserved.

Have evidence of a possible double standard?

Send the original source, the comparable case, and enough context for us to verify both sides. Do not send confidential or illegally obtained material.
